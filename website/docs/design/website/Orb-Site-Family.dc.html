For family and supporters

Setup takes an afternoon, together. After that, the phone belongs to the person using it.

Who they talk to

The people they see most, the places they go, and the phrases they use. Real photos, not symbols.

What works for their eyes and hands

Text size, contrast, theme, tile size, and speech rate, set inside Selvo. They can change any of it later.

Selvo offers. They choose.

Nothing is spoken that they did not pick. You help when they ask, not by default.

The afternoon, step by step

Do it side by side, with the person holding the phone. Ask before you add anything.
