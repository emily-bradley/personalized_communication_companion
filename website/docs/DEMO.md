# Selvo website demo

Live link: not deployed yet. Connect Cloudflare Pages using `docs/DEPLOY.md`; the address will be `https://selvo-website.pages.dev`.

## 5-minute walk-through

1. **Home, top of the page (1 minute).** The midnight hero: the wordmark, "Your voice. Your way.", and the glowing orb with the three sound bars. Point out that this is the only dark part of the site; everything after it is warm.
2. **Home, scroll (1 minute).** "Selvo helps them say what they mean", the three steps (Pick a picture, Choose your words, Speak), the phone showing three suggestions with Another, Incorrect, and Speak, the six "Built for adults" pillars, and "It started with Lucas".
3. **Our story (1 minute).** Lucas's story in four paragraphs, the mission band, and what Selvo is and is not.
4. **How it works (1 minute).** The three steps with app pictures, five places to start, Show it instead, and Made to keep up.
5. **For family and Articles (1 minute).** The afternoon setup in five steps, the questions families ask (tap one open), then Articles and the "three replies, not forty" article.
6. **Early access.** "Get early access" in the header jumps to the form on any page. Type an email and press Join the list to see "Saved. You are on the list."

## Things to know before you send it
- Photos of Lucas and a few FAQ answers still show as `[PLACEHOLDER]` text on purpose; the full list is in `docs/LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md`.
- Until `PUBLIC_WAITLIST_URL` is set, the form shows the success message but does not store the email.
- On a phone, the header wraps onto two rows. Everything else stacks into one column.
